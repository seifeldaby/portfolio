Who I Am

I'm an Electrical & Electronics Engineering student at Assiut University who genuinely loves the moment a circuit you designed on paper actually powers on. Over the last four years that's meant soldering 90+ PCBs by hand, teaching 150+ students how electronics work, and — most recently — building the autonomous stack that took my team to 7th place globally at ICRA 2026, in our very first year competing. I move comfortably between the hardware bench and the codebase: designing a board in Altium one day, debugging a ROS 2 SLAM pipeline the next.
